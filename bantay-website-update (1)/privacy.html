Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · matches the consent form in the app (version 4, November 2026)

The short version

  • Bantay-Kalusugan is a screening tool. It does not diagnose disease.
  • Your scores are calculated on your phone. Your answers and results are saved to our server only after you agree.
  • We collect your account details, your answers, including health information, and your results.
  • Your answers are used only for your own results. They are not used to train the model or for research.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with employers or insurance companies.
  • You can clear your saved results or delete your account yourself in Settings.

Who we are

Bantay-Kalusugan is an academic project by Bachelor of Science in Computer Science students at the College of Computer Studies and Systems (CCSS), University of the East – Manila, for the thesis “Explainable and Fair Machine Learning for Early Detection and Prediction of Chronic Diseases in the Filipino Population.” The research team is responsible for the personal information collected through the app.

Researchers: Ascue, Michael Vincent; Avellano, Charles Vincent; Fraco, Richard Andrei; Mendoza, Ernesto Jr.; San Jose, Steven Lee

What we collect

Health information is sensitive personal information under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, so we collect it only with your consent.

The app does not ask about your education, income or access to healthcare. It does not access your camera, contacts, photos, location or other files on your phone, and it uses the internet only to sign you in, send codes and save your results.

How your result is calculated

Your scores are calculated on your phone by a machine learning model trained on the answers of 391 Filipino adults who took part in our study's survey, so they work even without internet. The model does not learn from what you enter in the app. The result is a screening estimate, not a diagnosis.

How we use and share your information

  • To give you your results and explain which answers raised or lowered them.
  • To save your results to your account, or to your guest session, after you agree. If you are offline, they wait on your phone and are sent when you reconnect.

We do not use your answers to train the model or for research, and we do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with employers or insurance companies. Only authorized members of the research team can access it.

We use these service providers to run the app. Some of their servers may be outside the Philippines.

  • Supabase: the database where accounts and saved results are stored.
  • Render: hosts the server the app connects to.
  • Brevo: sends email codes; it receives your email address and the code.
  • textbee: sends SMS codes through an Android phone managed by the research team; it receives your mobile number and the code.

How we protect it

  • Information sent between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords and codes are never stored as plain text.
  • Your account can be signed in on one phone at a time: signing in on another phone signs out the first. Sessions also expire after 30 days, and resetting your password signs you out everywhere.
  • The database is protected by row-level security, and only authorized members of the research team can access it.
  • If you are offline, answers waiting to be sent are stored on your phone without extra encryption, so keep your phone locked.

How long we keep it

Your account and saved results are kept until the study ends, or until you delete them or ask us to, whichever comes first. They are then permanently deleted.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right:

  • To be informed: read this policy and the consent form in the app.
  • To access your information: see your past results in the app's History tab, or email us for a copy of everything we hold about you.
  • To receive a copy in a usable format: save a PDF summary of your results from Settings, or email us for a full copy.
  • To correct your information: take the questionnaire again, or email us.
  • To delete your information: clear your saved results or delete your account in Settings (a one-time code confirms an account deletion), or email us.
  • To object or withdraw your consent: email us. Withdrawing does not affect processing done before you withdrew.
  • To file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Who can use the app

Bantay-Kalusugan is for adults aged 18 to 65, the age range the model was built for. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Health notice

Bantay-Kalusugan is not a medical device and does not diagnose disease. Your scores are a screening estimate based on what you report. Consult a doctor or your barangay health center about your health. In an emergency, such as chest pain, difficulty breathing or sudden weakness on one side of the body, call 911 or go to the nearest hospital right away.
