Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version

  • We collect your name, mobile number or email, and your questionnaire answers.
  • Nothing from the questionnaire is saved until you give consent in the app.
  • Your answers are used to show your result and, with your consent, for our thesis research.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with insurers or employers.
  • You can delete your saved questionnaires in the app, and ask us to delete your account.

Who we are

Bantay-Kalusugan is an academic project by BS Computer Science students at the University of the East, Manila, for the thesis "Explainable and Fair Machine Learning for Early Detection and Prediction of Chronic Diseases in the Filipino Population." The research team is responsible for the personal information collected through the app.

The app does not access your camera, contacts, photos, location or files. It uses the internet only to sign you in and save your results.

How your result is calculated

Your risk level is calculated on your phone by a machine learning model trained on the answers of 391 Filipino adults who took part in our study. The result is a screening estimate, not a diagnosis.

Where your data is stored

We use these service providers to run the app. Some of their servers may be outside the Philippines.

  • Supabase: database where accounts and saved questionnaires are stored, over an encrypted connection.
  • Render: hosts the server the app connects to.
  • textbee: sends SMS verification codes through an Android phone managed by the research team. Your mobile number and the code pass through this service.
  • Brevo: sends email verification codes. Your email address and the code pass through this service.

If you are offline when you submit, your answers wait on your phone and are sent when you reconnect. While waiting, they are stored on your phone without extra encryption, so keep your phone locked.

Research use

If you consent, your answers may be included in our thesis analysis. Research results are reported only as group statistics. We never publish your name, number, email or individual answers.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until the end of the study, or until you delete it or ask us to, whichever comes first. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is processed;
  • access your data, and have it corrected if it is wrong;
  • object to processing, and withdraw your consent;
  • have your data deleted or blocked;
  • get a copy of your data in a usable format;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

You can delete your saved questionnaires anytime in the app under Settings. To delete your whole account or use any other right, email us at the contact above.

Age requirement

Bantay-Kalusugan is for adults aged 18 to 65. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger than 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
